Prana Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Effective: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what the Prana app does with your information. Prana is published by JusVibe ("we", "us"). It covers the Prana app for iPhone, the Prana app for Apple Watch, and Prana's widgets.

The short version

  • Prana has no account system of its own and no server of its own. Its social features use your iCloud account and Apple's iCloud storage for apps (called CloudKit).
  • Your session history, mood and focus answers, heart data and birth year stay on your devices. Prana does not upload them.
  • Prana has no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics. It does not send usage analytics anywhere.
  • If you join a circle, the people in it see a short record of each session: when it started (rounded down to the quarter hour), how long it was, whether you finished it, and the orb you chose if you share it. They do not see heart data or breathing measurements.
  • Find friends is off unless you turn it on, and only adults can use it.

1. What Prana is

Prana is a breathing and meditation app. It guides breathing sessions, can follow your breathing with your iPhone's or Apple Watch's motion sensors, can show how your heart rate changes using Apple Health, and lets you breathe with friends in small invitation-only groups called circles, or in live rooms.

2. No account, no server, no ads

  • No Prana account. You never sign up with us. Circles, rooms and find friends use the iCloud account signed in on your iPhone. Everything else works without it.
  • No server of our own. The only online storage Prana uses is Apple's CloudKit, part of iCloud. We do not run servers that receive your data.
  • No ads, tracking or analytics. Prana contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking code, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. Prana keeps a simple record of some app events (for example, that a session finished) on your device, and never sends it anywhere.
  • No diagnostic uploads. Prana does not send diagnostic or crash logs. Settings has an Export Diagnostics screen; a file there leaves your iPhone only if you choose to share it yourself.
  • Apple's own reports. If you have turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us. That is Apple's setting, not Prana's, and you can turn it off there.

3. What stays on your device

The following is stored only in Prana's own storage on your iPhone (shared with Prana's widgets) and, for the watch app, on your Apple Watch. Prana does not copy it to iCloud or to any device other than your paired Apple Watch (see below):

  • Your session history: date, time, length, exercise, and the measures worked out during each session.
  • Mood check-ins and answers to focus questions.
  • Heart data Prana reads from Apple Health, and results worked out from it (see Health and motion data).
  • Your birth year, if you give it (see Children and age).
  • Your settings, streaks, orbs, reminders and If-Then plans.
  • If you use find friends, the code made from your number and the last two digits of that number, so the app can show which number you listed.
  • A copy of your circles' information, so the Circles tab opens quickly.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, that backup can include Prana's data, as it can for any app. Apple handles device backups.

Between your iPhone and Apple Watch

Your iPhone and Apple Watch send session details to each other directly through Apple's watch connection: start and end time, length, exercise, average, lowest and highest heart rate, and how still you were.

Other features that run on your device

  • Insights. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Prana can use Apple's on-device language model to write insights about your sessions. This runs on your device.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and widgets. Prana offers shortcuts to start a session and to get insights, and widgets that show things like your streak and a suggested session. They use the data already on your device.
  • Camera. Prana uses the camera only to read a room's QR code when you choose to scan one. It does not save or send pictures.
  • Calendar. When you make an If-Then plan with the calendar option on, Prana asks for calendar access and adds an event named "Prana Session" with your plan's trigger in its notes. The event lives in your calendar like any other.
  • Contacts. If you allow access, Prana shows your contacts so you can pick friends to invite. Your address book stays on your iPhone, except for the specific details described under Circles (when you invite someone) and Find friends (when you look friends up).

4. What goes to iCloud and who can see it

Prana's social features store a small amount of data with Apple's CloudKit. It goes to one of three places:

  • Your private iCloud database. Circles you create are kept here and shared only with the people you invite.
  • Circles you joined. These live in the iCloud of the person who created the circle and are shared with its members.
  • Prana's public database. Rooms and find-friends listings are kept here. Any copy of Prana can look a record up, and we can see these records with Apple's CloudKit tools.

We cannot read your private iCloud data or what is shared in circles. Apple's CloudKit does not give app developers access to that data.

Several records use a hashed ID. iCloud gives Prana an ID for your account that is specific to Prana. Prana scrambles it with a one-way function (SHA-256) and uses the result to tell people apart. It is not your name, email address or phone number.

Circles

A circle is a private group of up to 8 people, including the person who made it. You can only join one by invitation. A circle stores:

  • The circle itself: the name its creator gives it, when it was made, the creator's hashed ID, and, if someone sets one, a daily ritual time with the hashed ID of whoever set it.
  • Your sessions: each time you end a session, Prana shares it with every circle you belong to. Each record holds the start time rounded down to the nearest 15 minutes, its length, whether you finished it, your hashed ID and the circle's ID. If Show my orb to my circles is on, it also holds which of the 7 orbs you chose and how far it has grown (0 to 4). No heart rate, breathing measurements, mood, notes or free text are shared.
  • Breathe-backs and nudges: which kind it is, the sender's and recipient's hashed IDs, when it was sent, and, for a breathe-back, which session it answers.
  • Weeks together: the week's start date and the hashed IDs of who started and who joined. These are not available to people under 18.

Who can see it: the members of that circle. iCloud's sharing also shows members each other's names, as iCloud provides them for shared items. The circle's creator can see who has been invited and has not joined yet.

Inviting people: when you invite someone from your contacts, Prana sends the phone numbers and email addresses of the people you picked to Apple's iCloud. iCloud uses them to find those people's iCloud accounts and add them to the circle. People iCloud cannot find are not added; you can invite them once they have Prana.

Block and Mute work only on your device. Nothing is sent, and the other person is not told. Someone you block can still see your sessions in circles you share. To stop that, leave the circle, or remove them if it is your circle.

Rooms (Breathe Together)

When you host a room, Prana saves a room record in the public database. It holds:

  • the 6-character room code
  • the title you type (letters, numbers and spaces only, up to 30 characters)
  • your hashed ID as the host
  • the start time, breathing pace and length
  • when the room was made

iCloud also notes which Prana user created a public record, using an ID specific to Prana. That ID is not your Apple Account email or phone number.

Who can see it: anyone who has the code or QR code. Because it is in the public database, any copy of Prana could look it up, and we can see it. Joining a room only reads its record.

How long it stays: Prana deletes the record when you, as host, cancel the room, leave it, or close Breathe Together. If Prana is closed before that happens, the record can stay in the public database. Prana stops letting people join a room once its session has ended.

SharePlay: if you are on a FaceTime call, a room can run over SharePlay. Then the room's details and, twice a second, your current point in the breath (a number between full exhale and full inhale) with a signal-quality number and a random ID for that session go to the other people on the call through Apple's SharePlay. Prana does not store this online.

Find friends (adults only, off unless you turn it on)

Find friends has two separate switches:

  • Let friends find me. You type your phone number. Prana saves a code made from it in the public database: a salted SHA-256 hash, not the number itself. Friends who have your number in their contacts then see that you are on Prana. Nothing else about you is shown. This code is not a strong lock: phone numbers are few enough that someone could try them all, and anyone who already has your number can tell you are on Prana. Numbers are not checked, so a match only ever says "on Prana". Turning this off removes the code.
  • Show which contacts are on Prana. Prana asks first. If you agree, it sends codes made from your contacts' phone numbers to the directory to see which are listed. Prana does not save these lookups online. Names and email addresses are not sent.

Who can see it: any copy of Prana can check whether a code is listed, which is how matching works. We can see the listed codes in the public database. Each listing also carries iCloud's Prana-specific ID for whoever created it.

5. Health and motion data

Prana asks for permission before it uses Apple Health. You choose what to allow.

  • What Prana reads: heart rate, heartbeat timing data and heart rate variability during sessions. On iPhone, for heart trends, it also reads resting heart rate and heart rate variability day by day (including the RMSSD measure on iOS versions that offer it).
  • What Prana writes: mindful minutes and workout records for your sessions, so they can appear in the Health app.
  • How it is used: only to show you how your heart changes during a session and from day to day.
  • Where it goes: nowhere outside your devices. Prana does not upload Health data to iCloud or anywhere else. It is not shared with circles or rooms. It is not used for advertising, and it is not sold.

Motion sensors: Prana uses your iPhone's or Apple Watch's motion sensors to follow your breathing and to tell how still you were. The readings are processed on the device as the session runs. Prana keeps only the results, such as a stillness level.

You can change Health access at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, then Health. Entries Prana wrote to Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.

6. Purchases

Prana Plus is a subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or billing details. Prana asks Apple, on your device, whether you have an active subscription. You can manage or cancel it in iOS Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions.

7. Notifications

  • Reminders (daily reminders, the evening reminder, a circle's ritual reminder and If-Then plan reminders) are scheduled on your device.
  • Circles: Prana asks iCloud to send your device a silent signal when something changes in your circles. Prana then fetches the change and shows any alert itself. Apple's push service and iCloud handle these signals. Prana does not upload a notification token to us.

You can turn notifications off in Prana's settings or in iOS Settings under Notifications, then Prana.

8. Reports and emails to us

  • Report: reporting a circle, a circle member or a room opens an email in your own Mail app, addressed to support@jusvibe.com. It is filled in with the circle's name, member's name or room's title, and a reference (the circle's ID, the member's hashed ID, or the room code). Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.
  • Contact Support: this opens an email filled in with the app version, your device type and iOS version. You can see and edit it before sending.

When you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to deal with your report. We keep these emails for as long as we need them to deal with your request.

9. Children and age

When you set up Prana, it asks for your birth year so it can choose age-appropriate settings. You can skip this and add it later in Settings. Your birth year stays on your iPhone. Prana uses it only for these settings and never uploads or shares it.

If you are under 18, or turn 18 this calendar year:

  • Find friends is not available.
  • Weeks together in circles are not available.
  • Energising breathing is not offered.
  • Friend streaks, sharing your orb with circles, and the evening reminder start off. You can turn them on.

If no birth year is given, find friends is not available. Other settings work as they do for adults. Circles are open to everyone, by invitation only.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child's use of Prana, email us at support@jusvibe.com.

10. How to delete your data

Prana does not have a single "delete everything" button. Here is what each option removes.

In the app

  • Session history: in the Trends tab, open History and swipe a session to delete it, or tap Edit. This removes the session and its focus answer from your device. It does not remove a session you already shared with a circle, or entries Prana wrote to Apple Health.
  • A session you shared with a circle: in the circle's feed, swipe your own session or press and hold it, then choose Delete. It is removed for everyone in that circle.
  • Leave a circle: open the circle and tap Leave circle. You stop seeing it, and new sessions are no longer shared with it. Sessions you already shared stay in that circle unless you delete them before leaving, or until its creator deletes the circle.
  • Delete a circle you created: open it and tap Delete circle. This removes the circle and everything shared in it, for everyone. It cannot be undone.
  • Find friends: in the You tab, open Find friends and tap Stop letting friends find me. This removes your code from the public directory. Turn off Show which contacts are on Prana to stop lookups.
  • Rooms: cancelling or leaving a room you host, or closing Breathe Together, deletes its record.

By deleting the app

Deleting Prana from your iPhone removes your history, answers, settings and birth year from it. Delete the watch app from your Apple Watch too. Deleting the app does not remove:

  • your find-friends listing. Stop find friends in the app before you delete it, because the app needs its stored code to remove the listing.
  • circles you created, or sessions you shared with circles, which stay in iCloud.
  • entries in Apple Health, or events added to your calendar.
  • a room record left behind if Prana closed before you left the room.

Through iCloud settings

iOS lets you delete the data an app keeps in your own iCloud. Open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then the option to manage storage (the names vary between iOS versions). Choose Prana if it is listed, and delete its data. Apple controls this screen. It is meant for data in your private iCloud, which includes circles you created; deleting it removes those circles for their members too. It does not cover records in Prana's public database, so stop find friends in the app first.

Ask us

If you cannot remove something yourself, for example a room or a find-friends listing left behind after you deleted the app, email support@jusvibe.com and we will look at what we can do. We cannot reach your private iCloud data or circles; use the steps above for those.

11. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Prana relies on Apple to work: iCloud and CloudKit (storage for circles, rooms and find friends), the App Store (purchases), SharePlay (live rooms on FaceTime) and Apple's push service (notifications). Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

12. Changes to this policy
