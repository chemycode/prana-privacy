Prana Support

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Prana is a breathing and meditation app for iPhone and Apple Watch. If something isn't working, or you have a question, we're glad to help.

Get help

Email us at support@jusvibe.com.

From the app: open the You tab, tap Settings, then Contact Support. This opens an email with the app version, your device type and iOS version already filled in, which helps us find the problem. You can edit it before sending. If your iPhone has no Mail account set up, Prana shows the address so you can copy it.

Frequently asked questions

Circles say I need to sign in to iCloud

Circles, rooms (Breathe Together) and find friends use your iCloud account. Prana has no account of its own.

  1. Open the Settings app and check that you are signed in with your Apple Account at the top.
  2. Check that you are online.
  3. Open Prana again and go to the Circles tab.

If Prana says iCloud is turned off for this device, iCloud is restricted on it, and circles are unavailable until that changes. Sessions, history and everything else in Prana work without iCloud.

Someone I invited to a circle wasn't found

When you invite people from your contacts, iCloud looks for their iCloud accounts. If it can't find someone, they aren't added. Once they have Prana and are signed in to iCloud, invite them to the circle again. A circle holds up to 8 people, including the person who made it.

How do I turn off reminders?
  • Daily reminder: You tab, then Settings, then Notifications. Turn off Daily Reminders.
  • Evening reminder: You tab, then Settings. Under Streak and reminders, turn off Evening reminder.
  • A circle's ritual reminder: open the circle and turn off Remind me under Ritual.
  • Alerts from one circle (sessions, nudges and breathe-backs): open the circle and turn on Mute alerts. The ritual reminder has its own switch.
  • Everything: in the iOS Settings app, tap Notifications, then Prana, and turn off Allow Notifications.
How do I restore my purchase?
  1. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.
  2. In Prana, open the You tab, tap Settings, then Subscription.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

Apple handles payments, billing and refunds. To manage or cancel your subscription, open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

How do I report a circle, a member or a room?
  • A circle (if you didn't create it): open the circle and tap Report near the bottom of its page.
  • A circle member: open the menu on their row in the circle and tap Report.
  • A room you joined: tap Report at the top right of the room's waiting room.

Each one opens an email to us in your Mail app, filled in with the name or title and a reference so we can tell which one you mean. Add what the problem is and send it. Nothing is sent until you send the email.

You can also Block a member from the menu on their row: you stop seeing their sessions, nudges and breathe-backs. Blocking works only on your device, and they are not told. They can still see your sessions in circles you share. To stop that, leave the circle, or remove them if you created it.

How do I delete my data?
  • Sessions on your iPhone: in the Trends tab, open History, then swipe a session to delete it.
  • A session you shared with a circle: swipe your session in the circle's feed, or press and hold it, then tap Delete.
  • A circle: open it and tap Leave circle, or Delete circle if you created it. Deleting removes it for everyone. Leaving keeps the sessions you already shared in that circle, so delete them first if you want them gone.
  • Find friends: in the You tab, open Find friends and tap Stop letting friends find me. Do this before you delete the app.

Deleting the app removes what is stored on your iPhone. It does not remove what is in iCloud or in Apple Health. The privacy policy explains each option in full.

How do I change what Prana can read from Health?

In the iOS Settings app, tap Privacy & Security, then Health, then Prana, and choose what Prana may read and write. You can also do this from the privacy section of your profile in the Health app. Prana never uploads Health data.

I can't find "Find friends"

Find friends is for people 18 and over. It appears in the You tab only when Prana knows you are an adult from the birth year you gave. If you skipped that step, add your birth year in the You tab under Settings, then Birth year. Your birth year stays on your iPhone.
